👆 Tap a coloured letter tile in the row below and choose its note length from the pictures — quaver, crotchet, minim or semibreve. Longer notes look wider; spaces stay as one‑beat rests.

Free play lets a child invent tunes from their own words; Follow along guides them to copy a sentence letter by letter. Either way, the keys sit on the real QWERTY keyboard and every note comes from one C‑major octave scale, so it always sounds musical while building typing and fine‑motor skills.
